Raidlands RoamBots victim-linked learning context - 2026-07-06

What changed:
- RaidlandsRoamBots is now v0.3.50.
- Player observation traces now distinguish passive nearest-bot samples from combat-linked target context.
- Player-vs-bot damage, kills, and deaths promote the actual tracked bot involved into the primary target fields.
- Fresh combat rows include target context fields such as `target_context_source`, `target_context_confidence`, `target_bot_key`, `combat_distance_to_target`, `combat_had_line_of_sight`, and combat exposure probe counts.
- Older/noisy rows are still usable, but model builds now weight low-confidence target context less heavily by default.
- `raidbots.learn report` and `raidbots.learn profile show` now expose target-context quality numbers for training-file review.
