Raidlands RoamBots sharper skill aim tuning - 2026-07-06

What changed:
- RaidlandsRoamBots v0.3.40 keeps bot hit damage at normal Rust weapon damage and makes misses real by applying a much tighter skill-scaled projectile aim cone.
- Skill `AimErrorDegrees` now actively affects fired bot projectiles instead of only existing in config.
- Bots still start a new aim warmup whenever they switch to a target, but the opening slop is much smaller and settles faster.
- Default v0.3.40 warmup tuning:
  - casual: 1.5 base degrees, +3.0 initial warmup degrees, settles over 2.5 seconds
  - average: 0.75 base degrees, +1.5 initial warmup degrees, settles over 1.75 seconds
  - dangerous: 0.2 base degrees, +0.4 initial warmup degrees, settles over 1.0 seconds
- The debug side panel now shows `Aim:` and `raidbots.list` now shows `aim=`, with current cone error and warmup progress.
